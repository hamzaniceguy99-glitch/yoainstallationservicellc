Our services, in detail

Four installation services for homes, designers and property managers, quoted after a free measure.

Program 01 · Fitted to the opening

Blinds, shades & shutters

No two openings in a house are the same width, and plenty are not square. We measure each one individually, at top, middle and bottom, decide inside or outside mount deliberately, and fit square to the window rather than to the wall that is out of true.

Program 02 · Anchored for the weight

Drapery rods & track hardware

Lined drapery is heavy, and a rod anchored only into drywall will pull out eventually. We locate studs or use the right anchors for the substrate, add intermediate brackets on long spans, and set heights so the fabric hangs level and clears the floor the way you intended.

Program 03 · On the wall, staying there

Wall decor, mirrors & shelving

Drywall, plaster, block and stud walls all hold weight differently, and a heavy mirror on the wrong anchor is a genuine hazard. We find out what the wall is first, set gallery walls out on paper before a single hole, and tell you honestly when something needs a different position.

Program 04 · Built right, once

Furniture assembly & fixtures

Flat-pack furniture is forgiving of nothing: one panel reversed early and the whole piece is wrong by the end. We build to the instructions, anchor tall units to the wall as the manufacturer requires, and mount televisions within the bracket's stated weight on fixings the wall can take.
