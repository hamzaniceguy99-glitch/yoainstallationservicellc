How we work

A Florida company, honest about the wall

YOA Installation Service LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, document number L23000351047, filed in 2023, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations. Insurance details are available on request before work begins.

We do not do electrical, plumbing or structural work — those go to licensed contractors, including hardwired motorised treatments that need an electrician. We also will not hang something heavy on an anchor that will not hold it, even when it is where you want it: we will explain the options instead, including moving it a few inches to catch a stud.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can you install blinds I already bought?

Yes, fit-only. We will measure the opening first and tell you honestly if what you have is the wrong size, because that is better found out before we start.

Do you do motorised window treatments?

Battery-powered ones, yes. Anything hardwired needs a licensed electrician, and we will coordinate with yours or refer you to one.

I have young children. What about cords?

Ask us for cordless or inaccessible-cord options and we will specify them. Corded coverings are a known strangulation risk for young children, and it is worth getting right.

Will you find the studs?

Yes, and if there is no stud where the item needs to go we use anchors rated for the weight with margin, or suggest moving the position slightly.

How long does a house of blinds take?

Usually one visit for an average home once the product is in. The quote states the expected time, and the measure is what makes that estimate reliable.

Are you insured?

Yes, with details available on request before work begins.

Got blinds sitting in boxes?

A free measure and an itemised written quote, with no obligation.
